How it works

1. Report

Post a lost or found item with a photo and details.

2. Search

Filter by name, category, status, date, and location.

3. Check matches

See suggested matches ranked by strength.

4. Claim

Send a claim request. Contact details stay private.

FindBack is an academic prototype. Data is saved only in this browser (LocalStorage) and is not shared between devices. There is no login or identity check, claim requests are simulated, and matches are rule-based, not AI-confirmed.
