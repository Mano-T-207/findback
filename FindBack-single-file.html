Lost Something? Let's Find It Back.

FindBack is a campus lost-and-found desk. Report what you lost or found, claim your item with proof, and collect it from the Admin Desk at a booked time.

Recently reported

How it works

1. Report

Lost: describe your item. Found: hand it to the Admin Desk and report it.

2. Claim with proof

Find your item under Found and send proof to the admin. Contacts stay private.

3. Admin verifies

The admin checks your proof, then approves or rejects and books a pickup slot.

4. Collect and return

Visit the Admin Desk at your slot with your ID. The admin marks it Returned.
